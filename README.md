MEET RECIPE COLLECTION!

THIS WEB IS ALL ABOUT CHECKING RECIPES OF FOOD, TRYING IT AND LEAVING YOUR IMPRESSIONS.

# HOW IT WORKS
-WHEN YOU ENTER THE WEBSITE, YOU WILL GET TO CHOOSE A CATEGORY OF FOOD.
-AFTER YOU CHOOSE THE CATEGORY, A TABLE OF POSTERS APPEAR! THESE POSTERS CONTAIN A ROLL OF RECIPE PAPER, OPTION TO ADD YOUR REVIEW AFTER YOU TRY THE RECIPE AND OPTION TO SEE OTHER PEOPLE'S REVIEWS.
-IN THE RECIPE PAPER YOU CAN READ THE RECIPE, MACROS AND ITS QUALITY, AND OF COURSE, YOU GET TO TAKE A LOOK AT THE FOOD YOURSELF!
-IF YOU CLICK THE "ADD YOUR REVIEW" BUTTON, YOU WILL HAVE TO ENTER YOUR NAME, YOUR RATING(1-5 STARS) AND A COMMENT REGARDING THE FOOD.
-IF YOU CLICK THE "SEE REVIEWS" BUTTON, YOU WILL SEE REVIEWS FROM OTHER PEOPLE AND YOUR REVIEW IF YOU ADDED ONE! NOW, IF YOU WANT TO CHANGE SOMETHING ABOUT YOUR REVIEW, YOU CAN CLICK THE "EDIT" BUTTON, BUT IF YOU WANT TO DELETE IT, YOU CAN SIMPLY PRESS "DELETE" BUTTON.
THATS IT!
